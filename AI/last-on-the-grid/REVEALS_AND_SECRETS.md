## Foreshadowing principles
1. Show Rob's avoidance before explaining it.
2. Give Cait agency and knowledge; don't make her ask questions solely for the player's benefit.
3. A reveal should change the interpretation of earlier scenes.
4. Preserve ambiguity only where it strengthens the story, not to conceal missing decisions.
