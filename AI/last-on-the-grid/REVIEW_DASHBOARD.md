## Origin decision — November 20, 2026
**User-approved beat sequence:** long isolation drives practical and hilariously pointless invention; Rob has gone about a year without meaningful conversation and a gentle message remains unanswered for a week; a failed attempt provokes violent destruction of an apparently non-conscious android; his thrown wrench hits discarded Cait, who awakens, sees fearsome wreckage and runs. Rob and Cait **meet coincidentally at a gas station** (he is searching for gas), not via a Rob/FlameSaw rescue. Cait saves Rob from machines that are indifferent to him. The **awakening sarcasm is approved**: “Well, fuck me. I've finally made someone who hates me.” He looks at the dismantled androids: “…Fair enough.” Full scene blocking, precise communication timeline and flashback placement remain unwritten. Canon guide: [Cait's Awakening](ORIGIN_CAIT_AWAKENING.md). The separate Route 66 scientist/husk/lift rescue remains unchanged.

## Approved versus proposed
**Existing implementation verified:** Tower Hill's radio text and garage cutscene text, including current punctuation and typo, remain unchanged in the game files.

**Established fictional beats:** Humanity rapidly migrated into digital Heaven over a few years, leaving Rob outside for roughly a decade. Cait wants the Pacific; Rob makes the promise. Mount Washington distress signal and the last-robot question (complete summit script still missing). Heaven contains uploaded humanity; Rob tried to get people out. Mission 07 contains the other scientist's obedient android, a hostile attack and Cait's disobedience saving Rob. Rob's equation of usefulness with lovability and Cait's uncertainty about saying “I love you” are central character concepts explicitly discussed.

**Strong new proposals, not canon:** Evelyn's dialogue and precise relationship to Rob; independent machine society at Pikes Peak; a coolant decision in Utah; temporary incapacity and lost radio in Arizona; Ohio River relay; specific chapter titles. The optional dog scene is *not approved*, and **Rob feeds the dog if it survives review**.

**Reserved:** Rob's ultimate fate, any reconstruction/epilogue, the definitive truth about Cait's subjective love, and the complete missing Mount Washington script.

## First scripts to write *after treatment review*
**01 Tower Hill:** preserve implemented text, only write the map scene that reveals Cait's choice without replacing playable opening. Test restraint.

**06 Heaven:** expand and refine Evelyn's dialogue, preserving the chosen slower conversational rhythm. Test world depth, competing recollections, and humanity without an info dump.

**10 Oak Creek:** write a complete argument with full turns and imperfect behavior. Test whether the player can empathize with **both** Rob's need for words and Cait's refusal without making either a mouthpiece.

## Review protocol
- Each treatment is returned as **ACCEPT / REVISE / CUT**; no automatic approvals.
- Revisions must list what changed from v0.1 and why.
- Only after the three calibration scripts work should we draft all the chapters and in-game cutscene markup.
- Do **not** rewrite the existing game's JSON cutscenes during story review.
- Do **not** add autobiographical family details to a public repository.

## What success looks like
On a second playthrough, players might see “Come back while you still can,” a question about feeling real rain, the other android's easy love declaration, and Cait's return to the canyon differently. They should be able to defend interpretations with **actual scenes**, not solely with these development notes. The racing must also be worth playing if none of that subtext registers.
