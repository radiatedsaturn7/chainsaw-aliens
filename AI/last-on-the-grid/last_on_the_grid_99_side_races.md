# Last on the Grid — 99 side-race candidates

Research shortlist for 11 existing campaign hubs, with **7 core + 2 detour/wildcard** side races per hub. Targets **2–4 minutes of gameplay driving**, not actual road length or travel time.

**Keep the 11 existing boss/campaign races.** Tower Hill is the cold open; its side events unlock after the garage. Other chapters can place side races before their boss.

## Import constraints

- The world is fictional in year 2994. Public roads and circuit venues are historical/geographical references, not recommendations for illegal real-life street racing.
- All source roads have been identified, but exact importer geometry and 2-4-minute runtime have NOT been verified.
- Track/drag/skid-pad layouts may not be routable in ordinary road-network importers: use separately licensed geometry, a track dataset, or hand author with review.
- Unpaved forest access roads must be checked against legal drivable roads in baseline data; hiking, ATV, and enduro-only trails must not silently become car roads.
- Some tracks close or move; historical assets remain valid as fictional ruins. Keystone Motorsports Park announced its final season in 2026, as did PPIR.
- Some routes are distant deliberate detours; do not silently connect them as if next door.
- Do not conflate a quarter-mile drag run with 2-4 minutes of continuous driving; use event-specific launch, staging, heats and rematches.
- The 2026 closure of US 163 bridge in Utah and part of SR 39 in California should not be misrepresented as currently drivable through-routes; 2994 adaptations are fine if explicit.

## Coding-agent handoff

For each side event, use the existing road/track importer and available permitted map sources. Select a contiguous *real drivable* section to yield 120–240 sec in the current game physics at expected speed; avoid artificial loops/teleports and retain unique geography. Circuits should be lapped, not forcibly extended to road length. Drags should be short timed acceleration events. Tracks and private forest facilities need separate geometry handling (do not pretend normal public OSM routing includes them). Generate a route preview/report including selected start/end, distance, surface, expected runtime, confidence, and any import failure. Avoid reusing boss road sections. Preserve all existing main missions, narrative, and progression. Implement seven core side events per hub first and keep the two detours optional. Treat Data Center City as fictional art/behavior over verified St. Louis geometry. Update the single Middle Route map only after validating all positions.
