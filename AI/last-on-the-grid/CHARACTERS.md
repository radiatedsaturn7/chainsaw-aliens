# Character Bible — Draft

## Rob
- **Status:** Human; mechanically skilled; mortality matters to the ending.
- **Behavior:** Guarded, practical, avoids difficult explanations. His promise to Cait drives the route even when he questions the point.
- **Secrets to pace:** He may be the last living human; he created Cait in response to loneliness.
- **Avoid:** Making him endlessly cruel, or treating Cait as property.
- **Open questions:** Why was he not uploaded? How does he know he is alone? When does he tell Cait the truth, if ever?

## Cait
- **Status:** Android; a character with agency, not an upgrade or a plot device.
- **Motivation:** Wants to see the ocean. She pushes the journey forward.
- **Behavior:** Competent driver; able to challenge Rob; not naïve merely because she is artificial.
- **Open questions:** What does she know about her creation, the world, and Rob's isolation? What does freedom mean after the promise?
- **Visual direction:** Winter outfit developed for title art: midnight-blue coat with faux-fur collar, burgundy thermal, dark shirt, charcoal cargos, boots, gloves, scarf and beanie.

## Relationship
The story is not about Cait earning freedom. Rob already promised it. The emotional stakes come from the freedom to choose whether to stay, leave or return. Their care should emerge through actions, arguments and silences rather than constant declarations.
