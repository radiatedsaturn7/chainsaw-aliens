REGIONAL CAMPAIGN MAP ARTWORK — DOT handoff

Create eleven DKC-inspired illustrated landscape backgrounds, keeping the game's
frozen post-apocalyptic setting. These are local journey maps, not satellite maps.
Use readable landforms and recognizable regional silhouettes with uncluttered
areas for the editable route overlay. Match the game's existing pixel artwork.

Regions / screen IDs:
1 campaign-hub-1: Providence / New England woods, lab outskirts, small city.
2 campaign-hub-2: Mount Washington, snow-covered mountains and alpine forest.
3 campaign-hub-3: Laurel Highlands, rolling forested ridges and old farmland.
4 campaign-hub-4: Ohio River, winding river valley, bridges and wooded hills.
5 campaign-hub-5: Ozarks, rugged wooded hollows, rocky clearings and dirt roads.
6 campaign-hub-6: Data Center City, abandoned automated industrial St. Louis.
7 campaign-hub-7: Route 66, open plains, mesas and abandoned roadside buildings.
8 campaign-hub-8: Pikes Peak, foothills rising to a dramatic snowy summit.
9 campaign-hub-9: Moki Dugway, desert mesas, canyon shelves and exposed cliffs.
10 campaign-hub-10: Oak Creek Canyon, red rock, wooded canyon and high plateau.
11 campaign-hub-11: Angeles Crest, Los Angeles outskirts rising into dry mountains.

Do NOT bake in roads, race nodes, labels, flags, cars or other UI. They are drawn
by the editable map widget. Backgrounds should work behind the existing nodes;
move nodes and curve handles in the Screens editor if composition needs it.
Suggested source: 1536 x 1024 PNG, with important scenery away from outer edges.
Check both portrait and landscape; the widget aspect changes with the screen.
Use the existing image-fit controls to choose the best framing.

Integration:
Save each image as an Art asset, e.g. campaign-map-providence.
Open Screens -> Game Menu -> campaign-hub-N -> regional-path world-map widget.
Set Widget background image (artRef), keeping the rest of the map intact.
The saved document is data/server-storage/files/menus/Game Menu/document.json.
Retain property=campaign-path, raceRef bindings, nodes, links and garage action.
Do not overwrite the whole menu from an older copy, regenerate the route menu,
or alter race files, unlock rules, car art or the geographic US overview.

Map authoring:
Edit nodes supports dragging races and junctions; Connect nodes makes roads.
Select a connection -> Connection style -> Road shape=bezier. Drag the two gold
handles, or set Bend 1/2 X/Y percentages. Paths use normalized map coordinates.
The existing miniature selected car follows those same curves. Branches can be
dotted. No separate car sprites or road-overlay bitmap is needed.

Current fallback backgrounds are procedural; each region's artRef replaces them.
