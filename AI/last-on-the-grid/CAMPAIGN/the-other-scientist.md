# The Other Scientist — Encounter Draft
> REVIEW DRAFT · Discussed November 10, 2026 · Location not locked

## Purpose
Contrast a scientist who built an android for obedience and personal gratification with Rob, who created Cait seeking genuine companionship and gave her the ability to choose and disobey. The payoff is not a speech about free will: Cait's disobedience **saves Rob's life**.

## Setting [PROPOSED]
A barn or other shelter discovered after a race, possibly in the Laurel Highlands of Pennsylvania. **The encounter is portable:** the story need not justify a particular race location. Keep the encounter separate from Data Center City.

## Sequence [ESTABLISHED STORY BEATS / ACTION DRAFT]
1. Rob and Cait stop at an abandoned shelter.
2. They discover a dead scientist on the floor. His android continues a repetitive sexual routine, mechanically obeying programming without understanding that the scientist is dead. The scene is grotesque and darkly comic, not erotic.
3. The other scientist did not consider free will; the android exists to fulfill his desires. Rob, by contrast, wanted a companion who could disagree with him.
4. Hostile husks/robots enter the building, forcing Rob and Cait to escape.
5. Rob orders Cait to close a door to stop the attackers.
6. Cait recognizes that the door will not hold. She **disobeys Rob** and instead uses a maintenance lift to block the corridor, saving him.
7. Rob initially objects to her deviation; then accepts that she was right.

### Attack / escape — action draft
**ROB:** “Cait, that door. Close it!”

*Cait runs another direction. A maintenance lift crashes through the ceiling, blocking the corridor and trapping the husks.*

**ROB:** “What the hell was that?”  
**CAIT:** “The door wouldn't have held.”  
**ROB:** “You could've bloody said something!”  
**CAIT:** “Didn't have time.”  
**ROB:** “…Fair enough.”

## Narrative notes
- Cait's choice is the **causal reason Rob survives** this attack.
- Don't turn the scene into an explicit lecture on free will.
- Do not confuse this attack with the earlier factory-escape/construction-robot sequence.
- Both discovery exchanges are **alternatives**, not sequential lines from one approved script.
- The quoted dialogue above was recovered in chat discussion, **not verified as verbatim from a saved screenplay**.
- The Laurel Highlands barn, exact choreography of the lift, and chapter placement remain editable.
