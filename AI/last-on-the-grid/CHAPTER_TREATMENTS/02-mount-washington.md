# Mission 02 — Pointless Detour
**Mount Washington Auto Road, New Hampshire · ESTABLISHED story premise; original summit dialogue incomplete**

## Narrative question
Why would someone travel far to confirm a disappointment they already expect? What does Cait understand about loneliness that Rob does not want discussed?

## Beginning
A mechanical distress repeater continues to broadcast from high on Mount Washington. Rob believes nobody is up there. Cait wants to investigate anyway, despite knowing the signal is probably automated. The remembered conversation includes Rob calling it “Your pointless detour.” There are versions of summit dialogue in prior chat history, but the full longer script has **not** been retrieved or verified. Do not treat a reconstruction as transcription.

Let the opening give the detour a lived motive, not a metaphysical one: Cait is annoyed that Rob has already decided the signal isn't worth following, while Rob resents the waste of fuel and the risk of the climb. He can be concerned and rude at once.

## Playing it
The mountain is a demanding ascent in winter—traction, crosswinds, visibility and vehicle temperature matter. The player physically commits to Cait's choice. Narrow roads and the possibility of retreat make her curiosity costly, but the challenge is compelling as driving, not merely as a corridor between cutscenes.

## Closing
An abandoned service area and a maintenance robot are found near the summit; the distress system has outlived whoever it was meant to reach. Earlier remembered versions include Cait switching off or silencing a machine and Rob saying they found nobody. Whether the robot was already inactive and the *transmitter* was what she stopped needs reconciliation before the scene is scripted.

On the descent, Cait asks what would happen if she were the last robot left. Rob responds with uncertainty and an apology; she apologizes too. Their exact original summit dialogue is **OPEN**. Keep their pauses, and preserve the possibility that Cait knows Rob is the last biological human while the player only suspects it.

## What each wants
Rob wants to return safely and avoid a topic he knows intimately. Cait wants to see the signal's source and may be trying to understand an experience he refuses to discuss.

## Consequence / next chapter
The mountain detour has consumed fuel and time. This makes the next leg through Pennsylvania practical, not a random geographic jump. Cait has learned that Rob will still accompany her on something that matters to her even when he thinks it is foolish.

## Second reading
This was never really a mission about whether a survivor would be rescued. It's about one person accompanying another to a place where they both expect to find nobody.
