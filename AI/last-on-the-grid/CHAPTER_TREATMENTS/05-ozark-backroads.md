# Mission 05 — Business as Usual
**Ozark Backroads, Missouri · World concept established; encounter NEW PROPOSAL**

## Narrative question
What does it mean to carry out a perfect service long after the people it was meant to serve are gone?

## Beginning
The route to the next supply network runs through an automated distribution district along the Ozark backroads. Trucks still circulate. Rail arms lift pallets. An electronic sign politely warns drivers their delivery is overdue by 3,470 days. A dispatcher offers assistance, provided they have an account registered to a human who has long since uploaded into Heaven.

Cait is fascinated by the engineering and annoyed by its rules; Rob likes an old-fashioned mechanical system he can outsmart. Their arguments are about *getting through*, not existential significance. Let them misread the system, get stuck, curse, and laugh.

## Playing it
A fast, hazardous race through a logistics network that isn't targeting the player. Timed gates, industrial vehicles and detours make an entertaining challenge. The obstacle isn't an evil factory boss: it's infrastructure running without anyone authorized to change a route.

## Closing
They reach a warehouse where automated equipment has stockpiled thousands of consumer goods nobody wants. The most absurd product is something mundane—coffee machines, perhaps. Cait has great fun at Rob's expense; he finds something genuinely useful in the scrap.

A worker machine reacts to Cait's request with cheerful procedural language. Cait tries asking it *what it wants*. It doesn't have the concept. She doesn't lecture it. She grows bored and goes to help Rob carry a part, then abruptly comes back and asks it another question. The moment unsettles her because it is so ordinary.

## What each wants
Rob wants a usable component and a way through. Cait wants to solve the ridiculous scheduling problem and is curious about the machines as neighbors, not curiosities.

## Consequence / next chapter
The functioning delivery system is maintained from an active computing complex. Following its service network or seeking a scarce part leads them to Data Center City. **Geography issue:** the current route puts Ozark Backroads before St. Louis, which can require backtracking east. Present this as a deliberate detour for the service network, or revise location order after map review.

## Second reading
The dispatcher can say “How may I help you?” indefinitely, without wanting anything. The distinction will become nastier in the other scientist's barn, and more personal when Rob mistakes his own usefulness for the reason Cait stays.

## Drafting caution
Do not make every working machine mindless; Pikes Peak later needs independent peers. Here the network's *particular architecture* is rigid, not proof that artificial minds cannot choose.
