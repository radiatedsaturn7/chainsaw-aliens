# Mission 01 — Come Back
**Tower Hill Road, Cumberland, Rhode Island · VERIFIED opening + PROPOSED connective scenes**

## Narrative question
Can someone want another person to come home without commanding them to? What does Rob's concern reveal before the player even knows what he is?

## Beginning / playable event
**Preserve the implemented opening.** Cait is bringing fuel back through a growing storm. Rob's radio text on staging is exactly: “Hey...”, “Winds shifted...”, “…Come back while you still can.” The race ends in completion or a time-out/failure that still advances to the garage. An anxious voice on a radio, not exposition, introduces their relationship.

The existing garage cutscene follows. It presently reads “Got the fuel...”, “Weather's getter worse...”, “Yeah...”, “I know...”, “We still heading out tomorrow?”, “That's what I promised...”, “Right...” (see Dialogue Archive for exact timings and the typo). Do not quietly insert the earlier scripted “Good.” into the implementation.

## Closing / new connective scene
The following morning, while packing, Cait unrolls a marked map. There is a hand-drawn line from Rhode Island toward California, with stretches marked “probably impassable.” Rob points out that they have an ocean nearby. She calls it the wrong ocean and gives him grief for being literal. He knows she made the decision a while ago, but the player is only now allowed into their conversation.

The exchange should meander: a disagreement about which car to take, a memory of a previous road, a joke about California, perhaps her excitement about physical ocean spray. Cait has been thinking of going for days or weeks. She has never stopped to choose a direction for *herself* instead of continuing whatever work needed doing; an old west-coast recording gave that desire a shape. She should sound restless, not like she's reciting the definition of free will. She tells Rob he needn't come. His reply is practical: the tires will need changing. On a second viewing this is both kindness and a reflex to make himself necessary.

**Protect:** This conversation is not the first scene of the game. The Tower Hill race stays the immediate gameplay opening. The decision has already occurred in story chronology.

## What each wants
Rob wants Cait home safely and then wants to keep the promise by doing what he knows: getting everything ready. He also enjoys her company and is uncomfortable naming how much. Cait wants a departure date that doesn't slip away, and to be taken seriously as someone choosing her own destination.

## Consequence / next chapter
Cait has a westbound map; Rob is committed to maintaining the journey. They hear a looping distress signal from Mount Washington—off the route. Cait wants to investigate. Rob calls it a pointless detour. This is their first disagreement about what the road is *for*.

## Second reading
“Come back while you still can” is a caring request, but later it may sound like an unconscious fear of being left. “That's what I promised” is both devotion and a shield against saying “I want to come.”

## Drafting caution
Avoid an early speech about isolation, design, or love. Make their prior familiarity tangible through a half-finished argument, tools in known places, and a private joke. Opening cutscene text remains untouched until explicitly approved.
