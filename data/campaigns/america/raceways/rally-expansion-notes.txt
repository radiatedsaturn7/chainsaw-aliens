Rally expansion: Sweden, Sno*Drift and New Zealand

Nine new standalone races. World Map > Rallies groups them into three additional
collections. They also appear in the geographic index (Michigan for Sno*Drift;
International for Sweden/New Zealand). No campaign progression changes.

Sweden — mapped-road recreations guided by official 2019 GPS controls:
  Vargåsen: approximately 14.3 km
  Torsby: approximately 9.2 km
  Likenäs: approximately 20.0 km
  https://rallysweden.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/gps-positioner.pdf
Checkpoint anchors are mapped road nodes within 60 m of the published controls.
Radio positions can be on side roads; blindly snapping to the closest edge caused
an access-spur detour on Vargåsen. Reviewed node anchors avoid that detour.
These are not claimed as exact historical competition geometry or surveyed jumps.

Sno*Drift — partial named-road sections from the 2025 organizer stage-map area:
  Agren: approximately 3.6 km (does not include Hunters)
  Shoreline: approximately 3.4 km (does not include Orchard)
  Old State: approximately 9.7 km (does not include Huff)
  Organizer reference is recorded in import_rally_collections.py and each race.
All six winter races have snow road surfaces and snow weather. They use existing
snow physics and saved art, with estimated widths and DEM terrain.

New Zealand — partial mapped-road sections:
  Whaanga Coast: approximately 18.2 km of Whaanga Road; no Ruapuke approach
  Te Hutewai: approximately 10.4 km gravel section
  Te Akau South: approximately 10.6 km gravel section
  https://www.hella.co.nz/assets/Raglan_Rally_of_the_Coast_-_Spectator_Guide_Map_and_Itinerary.pdf
Mapped surfaces are retained, including short paved portions on Whaanga Road.
These are not full WRC stages. No repeated passes count as separate stages.

Distances above are source geometry, rounded. Saved previews report playable
length after the shared importer places timing lines on suitable straights.

Reproducibility:
  tools/campaigns/import_rally_collections.py contains stage specifications.
  rally-road-sources.json records OSM query bounds and road filters.
  sources/*.json retains the actual imported geometry; each saved race records
  source snapshot/hash and organizer reference. Terrain uses Tilezen Terrarium.
  Rebuild using the existing import/install/finalize commands in the review JSON.

Deferred rather than guessed:
  100 Acre Wood: public spectator maps found; continuous stage trace unverified.
  Olympus: detailed organizer KMZ found was restricted to entrant recce use.
  Neither is represented as a newly playable collection.

Dedicated regional scenery is requested in ai/needed-graphics.txt. Current saved
fallback art is playable but does not reproduce every regional landmark/species.
