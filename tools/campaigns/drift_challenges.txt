Drift challenge provenance — 2026-11-21
=======================================
Uses existing OSM / Mapzen imported geometry; no replacement road geometry.
These are game-authored full-circuit score attacks, NOT official judged drift
sections, professional scoring rules, or tandem AI competition.

Horse Thief Slide: Willow Springs — Horse Thief Mile, 2 laps, ~3.22 km.
  Real drift use confirmed by organizer:
  https://sdrscca.com/willow-springs-weekend-2026/
  https://www.extremespeedtrackevents.com/3-este/general-info/17-htm-detail
Sideways Streets: Willow Springs — Streets of Willow, 2 laps, ~5.14 km.
  Selected as a varied technical game challenge on our existing imported layout.
  No claim that this is an official drift competition layout.
Atlanta Angle: Road Atlanta — Short Course, 2 laps, ~5.70 km.
  Formula Drift venue confirmed by organizer:
  https://news.formulad.com/2026/fd-news/2026-formula-drift-tickets-available-now/
  Our Short Course differs from the Formula Drift judged section.

Two-lap scores: bronze/silver/gold are authored starting targets (not calibrated
with an AI drifter). Each completed attack stores a profile personal best.
Scoring uses forward progress, slide angle, road contact and road boundaries.
Reversing to retry a corner, donuts, spins, airborne/off-course driving do not
award additional points. Race restart begins a new attempt.
Clear/daylight overlay applies only during the challenge; saved tracks are unchanged.

Geometry: OSM API snapshots retained in data/campaigns/america/raceways/sources.
Attribution: OpenStreetMap contributors; ODbL-1.0.
Elevation: GSI Japan 5 m DEM5A/DEM5B tiles (zoom 15) via the shared importer.
Attribution: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI),
https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html#dem
The adapter supports 10 m DEM fallback, but all ten final imports used 5 m data.
The initial coarse Mapzen pass was rejected after the grade audit.
Widths: preserve OSM width tags; otherwise documented conservative defaults.
Banking is not surveyed. Terrain elevation is approximate, not laser-scanned.
Control-point gap capped at 20 m and render surface step 2 m for tight layouts.
North, West, School shapes checked against operator PDFs; source-way alternatives
using pit lanes excluded. Touge retains the mapped connected loop, shorter than
the operator's stated course length; no artificial extension was invented.
Bihoku A/B lengths and outline identify each independently mapped course.
Mobara's real restricted drift zones are not reproduced: score attack uses the
full loop. Tsukuba is reversed to the operator's specified anticlockwise drift
direction, retaining the existing Course 1000 normal-direction race separately.
These are distinct layouts, not ten distinct venues. Shared regional art is used;
track-specific buildings, clipping zones and event judging markers are not surveyed.

Rebuild:
 python tools/campaigns/import_drift_courses.py
 node tools/campaigns/install_raceways.mjs --report-root=data/campaigns/america/drift-courses

Earlier candidate notes above are superseded for Ebisu and Tsukuba.
Meihan was also researched but its mapped training-maze connectors could not be
confidently identified as the C course; it is not installed under a guessed name.

Final GSI road-height audit is recorded in data/campaigns/america/drift-courses/elevation-audit.json.
Five-metre ground DEM still is not a surveyed racing surface. OSM centerline offsets,
road cuttings and embankments can affect the height profile; no artificial jumps
or forced length scaling are added. Medal targets are initial game calibration.
