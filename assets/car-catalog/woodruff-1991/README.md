# Woodruff 1991

Playable fictional 2994 restoration of the 1991 US Toyota Previa Deluxe: naturally aspirated 2438 cc 2TZ-FE, mid-mounted under the front seats, rear-wheel drive, five-speed manual, seven-passenger body and left-hand drive. This excludes LE, All-Trac, later supercharged versions and the narrower Japanese Emina/Lucida derivatives.

The US Toyota brochure lists 138 SAE net hp at 5000 rpm, 154 lb-ft at 4000 rpm and 3455 lb curb weight for the Deluxe manual column. It supplies 187/70.8/68.7 in length/width/height, 112.8 in wheelbase, P205/75R14 tires and 19.8 US gal tank. The source differentiates Deluxe rear drums from LE rear discs and manual from optional automatic weights. Original public brochure PDF, archive HTML and 18 public low-resolution page scans are retained. The PDF is one tall page concatenating 18 printed pages; printed 16 contains the table. No unavailable high-resolution scan is claimed.

The brochure verifies five-speed manual overdrive availability but omits individual reductions. The modeled 3.928/2.333/1.451/1/.851, reverse 4.743 and 4.3 final drive are explicitly unverified estimates. Auto Assist uses those manual gears and does not represent the optional four-speed ECT automatic. Cd, axle weight split, tracks, steering ratio, pressures, grip, RPM limits, performance targets, torque interpolation, suspension, losses and collision geometry are estimates in import.json. No passengers or cargo are added to curb weight.

Original badge-free 2994 artwork provides eight isolated exterior views with the existing animated wheel rig, a vehicle-specific curved beige cockpit, live speed/RPM/fuel/gear displays and a shared animated steering wheel. The initial atlas, spacing revision and final transparent atlas are preserved. The added RPM/gear displays are fictional aids. No manufacturer reference pixels are used as game art. The first wheel-coverage check caught a high far-wheel anchor; it was lowered to the opening center and all focused/browser/manifest checks repeated. Initial configuration, failing log and successful first browser evidence are retained alongside final evidence.
