# Scabious 2015

Original logo-free 2994 compact hot hatch inspired by the 2015 US Volkswagen Golf GTI S two-door, standard 210 SAE hp engine and six-speed manual. Performance Package, DSG, four-door and Golf R specifications are excluded. The original US brochure confirms trim distinctions. The OEM-attributed technical sheet hosted by ManualsLib and Manualzz supplies the two-door/manual dimensions, mass and physical gearing. These are rehosts of one source, not independent measurements. The brochure lists a general width; the technical sheet explicitly distinguishes two-door width and that value is selected.

Published physical gearbox ratios and both final drives are retained in import.json. The runtime supports one final drive: effective fifth/sixth ratios use an explicitly unverified shaft-allocation assumption. Auto Assist automates this manual. Factory progressive steering and XDS are approximated, and unsupported performance, suspension, differential and engine-curve settings are labelled estimates. No DIN/PS-to-SAE substitution or Performance Package power increase is used.

Both original generated PNGs and the first body sheet are preserved. The corrected body sheet supplies eight distinct directions; the first had repeated directions and is retained only for review. The vehicle-specific dashboard uses a shared moving steering wheel and fitted live speed, RPM and gear displays, styled as a 2994 salvage retrofit.

QA: 36 focused checks and four browser checks passed, followed by five focused checks and a fresh eight-view browser pass after correcting the left rear wheel center. Initial and corrected fit screenshots are retained. Manifest validation covers 1,859 documents and 16 assets. QA logs and screenshots are in qa/.
