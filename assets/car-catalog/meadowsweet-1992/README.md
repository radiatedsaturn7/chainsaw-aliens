# Meadowsweet 1992

Original logo-free cream/forest-green 2994 hatchback referencing the **early-1992 French-market LHD Lancia Delta HF Integrale Evoluzione I, 1995 cc turbo 16V, five-speed manual**. This is not Evo II or the Swiss-market 8V Kat. Non-catalyst specification is the selected trim assumption: the inspected brochure does not explicitly certify catalyst absence, so no emissions/homologation claim is made. Gearing and unsupported chassis values remain estimates.

The [April 1992 manufacturer brochure](https://www.brochure-catalogue-voiture-neuve.fr/brochures/lancia/delta/lancia-delta-1979/FRA-04-1992-HF-INTEGRALE/) was independently downloaded through its inline preview link and visually inspected. Technical PDF page 17 confirms 205 ch CEE / 151 kW at 5750 rpm, 298 Nm at 3500 rpm, 1300 kg “poids en ordre de marche,” 205/50 ZR15, 3900 mm length, 1770 mm width and 47:53 front/rear torque distribution. Page 18 carries the IV/92 imprint. The brochure's running-order mass is retained without inventing its driver allowance. Common 210 PS figures are not substituted for this period CEE rating. The original PDF remains outside the repository at `/tmp/meadowsweet-brochure-preview.pdf`; its SHA256 and inspected pages are in `prompts.json`.

The imported scalar power converts 151 kW to mechanical hp (native rounded 202), and torque converts 298 Nm to lb-ft (220). Nominal tire radius is calculated as 0.293 m. Five forward ratios, reverse and final drive are explicitly estimated; Auto Assist automates that modeled manual gearbox. Wheelbase, tracks, height, torque-curve interpolation, idle/redline, aero, suspension, brakes, differential locking, tire pressures/grip, body profile and efficiency are estimates individually qualified in `research.json` and the runtime provenance. No measured acceleration or top-speed certification is claimed.

Both PNGs are original image-generation outputs copied without editing, with original files retained under `/workspace/generated_images/`. Eight individually mapped exterior views use measured transparent wheel openings and existing shared tire art. The vehicle-specific LHD dashboard uses the shared rotating steering wheel, with live speed/RPM/gear and fuel readouts in the fitted instrument opening. All eight production exterior composites and desktop/handheld cockpit captures were visually reviewed. The minor wheel-opening correction changes graphical coverage only.
