# Fumitory 1974

Playable fictional 2994 restoration of the US-market 1974 Saab Sonett III: 1698 cc V4, four-speed manual, front-wheel drive, left-hand drive, standard cast 15-inch wheels and no optional factory air conditioning. The original Saab-Scania of America brochure supplies 65 SAE net hp at 4700 rpm, 85 lb-ft at 2500 rpm and 1900 lb curb weight without AC. Its 1970-lb AC variant is excluded. This is the large-impact-bumper 1974 version, not an earlier small-bumper or 1500 cc variant.

The brochure gives overall engine-to-wheel reductions of 18.2/9.7/6.0/3.9 and reverse14.8, plus a4.67 axle. Individual gearbox ratios are derived by division from those rounded published numbers; they are not independently verified tooth-count ratios. Auto Assist is a fictional driving aid using these manual ratios. The source's freewheel is modeled locked for conventional engine braking. No global drivetrain behavior was changed.

Source PDF and all five rendered pages are preserved in references. Metric body length4065mm is used because the paired printed159.8in differs slightly. Cd0.31 is sourced; 165/80R15 approximates the brochure's165SR15 designation. Front weight fraction, tire pressure, tank capacity, steering ratio, performance targets, RPM limits, interpolation, suspension, losses and collision geometry are explicit estimates in import.json.

Original badge-free artwork includes eight distinct views, a fitted three-dial black-vinyl and wood-trim cockpit, live speed/RPM/fuel/gear and the shared animated steering wheel and tire rig. The first atlas and corrected opposite-side quarter views are preserved alongside the final body. Reference pixels are not used in game art.

Validation evidence in qa covers29 focused unit checks, manifest validation, keyboard and handheld touch movement, all eight composited views and the cockpit. The real static picker check verifies an initial visible entry and its art, navigates to Fumitory on a later page, waits for exterior art, opens the cockpit and drives. Motion evidence establishes actual input and movement, not measured real-world performance.
