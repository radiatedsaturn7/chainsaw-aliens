# Santolina 1967

Original badge-free 2994 interpretation of the Japan-market 1967 Honda N360 standard two-door sedan: 354 cc forced-air-cooled SOHC twin, one carburetor, front-wheel drive, four-speed manual and right-hand drive. Excludes S/M/G equipment packages, 36 PS T-series, N600, LN360 van, automatic and sunroof versions.

## Evidence and units

The saved Honda model archive identifies the March 1967–December 1968 model. Its base technical sheet supplies 31 PS at 8500 rpm, 3.0 kg-m at 5500 rpm, 475 kg vehicle weight, 26-litre tank, dimensions, steering ratio and transmission reductions. The original page retains a type-approval-pending footer; this is not silently treated as a final homologation document. Honda's production history confirms the March 6, 1967 sales launch, and the engineering society's record of the actual 1967 production vehicle at Honda Collection Hall independently corroborates the engine, power, mass, dimensions, FWD and four-speed layout. The archive's separate trim page prevents mixing in later automatic or T-series specifications.

Runtime converts metric PS to approximately 30.576 mechanical hp and kg-m to 21.699 lb-ft, summarized as 31 hp/22 lb-ft. No SAE, DIN or CEE power test standard is inferred. The source calls 475 kg vehicle weight without an explicit fuel/driver convention; no DIN mass definition or additional driver is invented.

The sourced gears are 2.529/1.565/1.000/0.605, reverse 2.055. Both the 2.812 primary chain reduction and 3.739 secondary helical reduction must apply. The existing single final-drive field stores their product, 10.514068. This is a combined effective reduction, not a claim of a 10.514 physical axle. No global drivetrain changes. Auto Assist is fictional assistance on these manual gears, not the factory automatic introduced in 1968.

## Explicit estimates and fidelity limits

Original 5.20-10-2PR tires are represented by an estimated 135/80-10 equivalent and 0.235 m radius in the metric-only editor. Neither the aspect ratio nor loaded rolling diameter is verified; this does not claim factory radial tires. Front weight distribution, drag, CG, overhang split, colliders, tire grip/pressure, brake bias, losses, suspension, torque interpolation, idle/redline/rev limit and shifts are modeling estimates. The manufacturer's 115 km/h claim is a target, not a calibrated simulation result; 29-second 0–60 is an estimate. Generic suspension does not reproduce leaf-spring friction or drum-brake fade.

The original yellow body and simple metal/vinyl RHD dashboard are a fictional 2994 restoration. Added live RPM, gear and fuel instruments are restoration equipment, not a claim that the base car had the S/G sports meter. Shared animated steering and tires and generic sound remain. No manufacturer photo pixels or logos enter production art.
