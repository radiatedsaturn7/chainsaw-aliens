# Osier 2005

Playable fictional 2994 restoration of a documented US 2005 Dodge Dakota SLT Quad Cab 4WD short-bed configuration: standard-output 4.7-litre EVA V8, 545RFE automatic, 3.55 open axle, optional NV244HD and standard P245/70R16 tires. This is a selected production variant, not a claim to identify Rob's personal truck. It excludes the HO engine, ST/Laramie trim, Club Cab and NV233 transfer case.

Dodge's original product specifications confirm 230 SAE net hp at 4600 rpm, 295 lb-ft at 3600 rpm, a 5750-rpm maximum, five upshift ratios of 3/1.67/1/.75/.67, the 3.55 axle, 22-US-gallon tank and 17.4:1 steering. The detailed Quad Cab SLT 4WD automatic table supplies a 4696-lb base curb mass. That table does not isolate optional NV244 equipment mass; this is therefore a simulation baseline, not an exact option-equipped measured weight. Its printed axle values total 4697 lb; front mass fraction uses 2750/(2750+1947), preserving that rounding discrepancy.

The owner manual confirms normal full-time AWD allows different front/rear shaft speeds and also describes selectable locked high, locked low and neutral. Gameplay represents **normal full-time high range only**, using the existing open-center AWD model and the sourced 48:52 front/rear split. Locked high/low selection, neutral, driveline wind-up and exact transfer-case dynamics are not simulated. The decorative lower dashboard dial is not interactive. No global transfer-case controls or runtime changes were added.

The automatic uses the five upshift ratios. Its alternate 1.50 second-gear kick-down path is not separately modeled. Reverse ratio, converter parameters, shift timing, idle/redline, curve interpolation, performance targets, pressures, grip, suspension and collision geometry remain explicit estimates. Cd .457 is itself labeled an estimate by the manufacturer. Curb mass excludes added cargo/occupants. Original PDFs, extracted text and relevant rendered pages are retained under references; no OEM pixels are used in game artwork.

Original logo-free dark-red artwork supplies eight distinct exterior views, fitted animated tire layers, a vehicle-specific dark-slate cockpit with white gauge faces, live speed/RPM/fuel/gear and the shared animated steering wheel. Both initial and corrected atlas originals are preserved. The unbranded grille and 2994 wear are fictional.

QA covers 30 focused tests, including native editor roundtrip, automatic gearing, open-center AWD semantics, roster identity, wheel openings and force allocation. Browser checks exercise moving keyboard and handheld touch input, eight editor composites, fitted live cockpit and the real static car picker from initial visible entry through a later page to Osier cockpit driving. Manifest validation passes for 1919 documents and 16 assets. Preservation checks are recorded in qa. Gameplay movement is not a measured real-world acceleration claim.
