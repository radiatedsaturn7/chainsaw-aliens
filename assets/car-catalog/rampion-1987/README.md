# Rampion 1987

Local-only Legends tribute to Roberto Ravaglia's BMW M3 E30 Group A at the fourth round of the 1987 WTCC, Nürburgring. BMW's event-specific photo caption establishes that appearance; its manufacturer history and museum confirm the world title. This is not a claim that he won this particular event. No chassis serial, exact race number, co-driver or event-day setup is invented. Not the road Ravaglia edition or later 2.5-litre DTM evolution.

## Evidence and modeling boundaries

- BMW German 2012 history gives the contemporary competition 2.3-litre naturally aspirated four up to 300 PS at 8,200 rpm. English 'hp' is not treated as mechanical horsepower: 300 PS = 295.896 mechanical hp. Museum listing instead says 220 kW/300 hp at 8,000 rpm; the press-release metric rating and rpm are chosen explicitly, not blended into a fictional exact dyno.
- BMW museum gives 2,332 cc. Original FIA A5327 page 2 gives 2,302.1 cc baseline and 2,331.8 cc maximum allowed. The rounded museum competition reference is used, not a claim of measured event bore/stroke. No 2.5L/355PS late specification.
- FIA form pages 2, 6 and 7 visually inspected: 4,355 × 1,680 mm, 2,565.5 mm wheelbase, 789.5/1,000 mm overhangs; Getrag five-speed manual options; rear hypoid limited-slip drive. These are homologation references, not a Nürburgring setup sheet. All five ratios and final drive are simulation estimates; no specific event gearbox internals claimed.
- Running mass 1,040 kg, torque curve apart from period maximum context, 210 lb-ft peak, idle/limiter, 165 mph and 5.5-second 0–60 targets, 225/45R16 tires, 100L tank, 8 MPG, tracks, height, steering, grip, aero, brakes, suspension, differential and geometry beyond the homologated dimensions are labeled estimates. These targets are not claimed measured performance. Generic native engine sound and shifting do not reproduce a specific S14 recording or dog-leg gate. Auto Assist is accessibility only.

Exact primary URLs and field-level provenance live in `import.json`. No source photographs or manufacturer livery assets were imported. The FIA PDF is read-only QA evidence outside this repository, not a game asset.
