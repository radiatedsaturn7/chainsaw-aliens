# Calamint 2003

Original badge-free 2994 interpretation of the US-market 2003 Subaru Baja standard launch model: naturally aspirated 2.5-litre SOHC flat-four, five-speed manual, full-time AWD and four-seat open-bed body. Excludes the later Sport/Turbo, optional automatic and STX concept.

## Period evidence and estimates

The original Subaru of America brochure MSA 8M0311 is preserved as all 30 publicly available page scans from ImportArchive. Pages 13, 19 and 26 establish drivetrain differences, cockpit design and specifications. Its indexed transcription provides a legibility crosscheck. The public scans were used without accessing the site's paid high-resolution download.

The manufacturer lists 165 horsepower at 5600 rpm and 166 lb-ft at 4000 rpm. It does not print an explicit SAE test-standard suffix; no PS or SAE gross conversion is invented. Published displacement remains 2.5 litres without an unsupported precise engine-code claim. Manual curb mass is 3485 lb (1580.7694 kg), distinct from the automatic's 3550 lb. No driver or cargo is added.

Dimensions, 225/60R16 tires, 7.3-inch curb clearance and 16.9 US-gallon tank follow the brochure. Five forward speeds, a viscous-coupling center differential and rear limited-slip differential are sourced. Individual forward/reverse reductions and final drive remain unverified estimates. Auto Assist is fictional assistance on the manual gearset, not the factory four-speed automatic.

The existing AWD model uses an estimated 50:50 center balance and mild rear limited-slip settings. It does not simulate temperature-dependent viscous coupling behavior. Weight distribution, steering ratio, torque interpolation, pressures, CG, suspension, colliders, overhang split, losses, idle/rev limits, shift settings and performance targets are estimates. There are no new global drivetrain controls.
