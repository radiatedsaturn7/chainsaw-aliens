# Anemone 1986

Original logo-free 2994 mid-engine fastback inspired by the early 1986 US Pontiac Fiero GT, four-speed manual with rear wing. Not the later five-speed, 1988 suspension, notchback or a swapped-engine build.

The retained GM Canada GT brochure corroborates the four-speed drivetrain, staggered 205/60 R15 front and 215/60 R15 rear tires, dimensions, and cockpit layout. US road-test specification and measured mass are attributed separately in import.json; this is not a claim of exact Canadian factory kerb weight. The 1988 leaflet examined during research was rejected for model/mass ambiguity. Gear ratios and unsupported simulation settings are explicit estimates. Auto Assist automates the four-speed manual.

The original body and dashboard PNGs are retained with generation prompts. Rear-quarter source images were mapped according to their actual orientation rather than their requested grid positions. Eight production editor views and desktop/handheld moving cockpit screenshots were visually reviewed for fit; live speed, RPM and gear are 2994 retrofit gauges. The car uses shared moving tire and steering-wheel layers.

QA: 36 focused drivetrain/catalog checks, three production browser checks, and manifest validation passed (1,856 documents, 16 assets). Moving keyboard and touch telemetry and screenshots are preserved in qa/. Straight test roads are in memory only; no saved race or runtime file changed.
