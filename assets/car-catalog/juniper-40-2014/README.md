# Juniper 40 2014

Original badge-free 2994 utility car, mechanically referencing the US 2014 Toyota FJ Cruiser standard 4x4 6MT in high range. Not the Trail Teams edition or factory automatic. Original unchanged generated body/dashboard PNGs; eight native exterior frames, shared wheels, fixed rear camera, existing surface effects, fitted shared steering wheel and live speed/RPM/gear/fuel gauges.

Toyota's [manufacturer release](https://pressroom.toyota.com/2014-toyota-fj-cruiser/) establishes the engine, six-speed full-time four-wheel drive, normal 40:60 split and 3.909 manual final drive. The [archived manufacturer brochure](https://cdn.dealereprocess.org/cdn/brochures/toyota/2014-fjcruiser.pdf), page 11, supplies dimensions, tracks, 19-gallon tank, P265/70R17 tires and engine peak RPM. Its 4332 lb manual curb mass is used (1964.962 kg); secondary reporting of later product information gives 4290 lb, but that primary PDF could not be accessed. The discrepancy is explicitly unresolved, not silently averaged.

The six individual high-range ratios and reverse are game estimates; Auto Assist automates the same manual set. Normal AWD rear share is 0.6. Selectable low range, center/rear locks, calibrated Torsen, A-TRAC and crawl control are not simulated. Stock M+S tires use the existing street compound approximation. Redline, cutoff, curve interpolation, mass distribution, steering, drag, suspension, grip and displayed performance figures are game tuning; see research.json. No production-world files are changed.

Validation: native import and editor roundtrip, source hashes, eight unique frames, source/published camera and smoke parity, wheel opening coverage, actual moving Car Editor production-physics previews at desktop/handheld sizes, and project manifest asset validation. These are real game renderer previews, not a full campaign driving acceptance.

Runtime contact regression: the initial solid body collider occluded all tire contact paths. The existing wheel-well-aware compound collider now represents the body openings; a flat-road positive/negative test proves launch and preserves rejection of the obstructed configuration. CG height is consistently estimated at 0.72 m in suspension and collision geometry. The factory front double-wishbone/rear solid-axle architecture is recorded, but Car Editor does not forward per-car suspension geometry. Runtime therefore retains generic MacPherson/multilink presets, a documented approximation; no engine changes are included.
