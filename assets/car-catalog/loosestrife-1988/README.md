# Loosestrife 1988

Original badge-free 2994 interpretation of the French 1988 Peugeot 405 Mi16 Phase 1 four-door saloon: naturally aspirated 1905 cc sixteen-valve engine, 10.4:1 compression, front-wheel drive and five-speed manual, early 195/60 VR14 tires. It excludes the US export, Swiss catalyst, Mi16x4, later two-litre, Le Mans and T16 variants.

## Source and modeling boundaries

The original French 1988 Peugeot brochure is preserved from two public hosts, with complete pages and rating-detail crops. Its Mi16 page supplies body/cockpit references; its technical page explicitly separates CEE and DIN ratings. Peugeot's own historical retrospective confirms the early 1.9/160 CV model and the later 15-inch, AWD and two-litre catalyst changes.

- The simulation uses the paired DIN figures: 160 metric ch at 6500 rpm and 18.4 kgm at 5000 rpm. They convert to about 157.81 mechanical hp and 133.08 lb-ft; native editor summaries round to 158 and 133. The separate 116 kW CEE figure is retained as a distinct rating, not falsely presented as the conversion of 160 ch DIN. No SAE gross/net or US-market values are substituted.
- The brochure gives 1100 kg **poids à vide**, without a clear fuel/driver convention. Runtime 1125 kg is an explicit modeling allowance of 25 kg above that empty figure, not a verified curb-mass claim. No driver is added.
- The French high-compression specification is interpreted as non-catalyst from the period market and maker chronology. Exact catalyst hardware or engine-code certification is not claimed.
- Five speeds are sourced; individual reductions, reverse and final drive are explicitly unverified estimates. Auto Assist uses the modeled manual gears and is not a factory automatic.
- Approximate dimensions, handling, pressures, collider geometry, torque interpolation, idle/rev limits, shift behavior and 0–60 time are estimates. Period 220 km/h and 8.6-second 0–100 targets are not independently calibrated results.
