# Camomile 1988

Original logo-free 2994 saloon inspired by the France model-year 1988 Citroën 2CV 6 Spécial, 602 cc, four-speed manual, left-hand drive and closed fabric roof. Not the Charleston, Dyane, 435 cc 2CV 4, or a mixed UK model-year specification.

The retained Citroën France brochure's technical column (PDF page 4, printed page 6) specifies 602 cc, four speeds, 21 kW ISO at 5,750 rpm, separately printed 29 ch DIN, front discs/rear drums, 125 R15 X tires, 115 km/h and a 25-litre tank. Its final page explicitly identifies model year 1988. The simulated 585 kg is derived from the stated 930 kg maximum weight less 345 kg payload including driver. It is not an independently published kerb measurement. Mechanical horsepower is converted from 21 kW; the ISO and DIN numbers are not treated as numerically identical, and no SAE-gross rating is used.

The 39 Nm torque, four individual ratios, final drive, assumed 80-percent tire aspect ratio, dimensions, acceleration and other unsupported simulation values are explicitly estimates in import.json. Auto Assist operates the four manual ratios, not a factory automatic.

body.png and dashboard.png preserve original generated art. Eight native exterior angles have measured wheel openings and shared moving tire layers. The vehicle-specific painted-metal dashboard has its characteristic shelf and dash-mounted gear lever, with a fitted shared steering wheel and live speed/RPM/gear instruments as a 2994 retrofit. No manufacturer logos are used. references/ preserves the period PDF and readable technical/model-year pages. qa/ contains production editor views, real keyboard/touch driving screenshots and telemetry, focused tests, manifest validation and file hashes.
