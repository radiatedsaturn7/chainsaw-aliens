# Alyssum 2009

Original badge-free 2994 interpretation of the 2009 US smart fortwo pure coupe (451), 999 cc naturally aspirated gasoline triple, rear engine, rear-wheel drive and five-speed automated manual. This is not the roadster, earlier 450, passion, BRABUS, cabriolet, diesel or electric version.

The original smart USA 2009 brochure is retained in references with its technical and pure-trim pages. It gives 70 net hp at 5800 rpm, 68 lb-ft at 4500 rpm, 1808 lb coupe curb mass without driver, 155/60 R15 front and 175/55 R15 rear tires. Black structural cell, solid roof and standard steel-wheel styling follow pure trim. Optional clock/tachometer pods are omitted. The small live RPM display is fictional restoration equipment.

Individual gear ratios, final drive, shift timing, torque interpolation, losses, suspension, tire grip, mass distribution, aerodynamic coefficient, rev limits and collision geometry are explicitly estimated in import.json. Existing automatic controls with zero converter slip approximate AMT automatic mode; factory sequential controls and control maps are not reproduced. Manufacturer 90 mph limited maximum and 12.8-second 0–60 figures are targets, not independently calibrated results.

The authored wheelbase is the brochure’s 73.5 inches (1.8669 m). Existing runtime physics clamps wheelbase to 2.1 m. This limitation is retained without global changes, so precise turning behavior is not claimed. The fitted collider uses an estimated .49 m body CG and .16 m clearance; its separate runtime CG follows the existing engine. The initial estimated collider had no valid tread contact, caught by real driving QA. Its config, failed browser logs and telemetry are preserved. Corrected production-physics launch is covered with traction control both enabled and disabled.

All three generated PNG originals are retained byte-for-byte, including the first body revision. The corrected transparent atlas provides eight distinct views. Production art contains no manufacturer logos or source-image pixels. Live speed, RPM, gear and fuel fit the vehicle-specific dashboard; shared tire and animated steering-wheel layers use existing game behavior.

Focused tests cover native roundtrip, source boundaries, roster identity/count, eight frames, hashes, wheel coverage and sustained launch. Browser tests exercise actual keyboard and touch driving, exterior and cockpit rendering, eight camera views, and the real static picker from its initial visible entry to a later page with loaded Alyssum art, then cockpit driving. Test roads exist only in memory; no saved race content changes.

This is a local-only addition. The older 104-car branch remains separate. Existing cars, races, refs, originals and QA remain unchanged. No runtime/world changes, publication, remote writes, transfers or deletion.
