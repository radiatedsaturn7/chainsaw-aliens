# Quince 2014

Original logo-free 2994 salvage micro-hatch, with native eight-angle body art and individually fitted RHD cockpit. Real-world reference is the Japanese April 2014 Toyota iQ 130 G MT, not the stronger UK iQ 3 or CVT version. Shared runtime tires and steering wheel, fixed-rear camera and surface smoke are retained.

Toyota Japan's model catalog anchors 950 kg,69 kW at 6000 rpm,118 Nm at 4400 rpm,1329 cc,32 L,175/65 R 15,2 m wheelbase and FWD 6 MT. The official May 2015 specification sheet corroborates the same 2014–2016 manual variant and supplies its six forward ratios, reverse 3.333, final 3.736 and 135 mm clearance. See import.json provenance for URLs and explicit estimates.

No brand logos or borrowed manufacturer artwork. Source PNGs generated with built-in imagegen; prompts.json records the complete prompts. Quince is a fictional design with a broadly similar packaging concept, not a replica.

Geometry, non-anchor torque values and performance displays remain game estimates. Torque/power/curb mass are not licensing or exact dynamic-equivalence claims. Native editor roundtrip, production launch, wheel coverage and aggregate tests are required; exact-head browser CI and actual eight-angle/390/1280 cockpit visual review remain mandatory before release.
