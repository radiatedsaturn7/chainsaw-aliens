# Glimmer 1986
Original logo-free 2994 salvage mid-engine wedge, informed by the 1986 US naturally aspirated MR2 five-speed. Eight original exterior views and an individually calibrated LHD cockpit use shared runtime tires and steering wheel, fixed rear camera and surface smoke.

The archived OEM US brochure supplies 112 SAE net hp at 6600 rpm, 97 lb-ft at 4800 rpm, 2291 lb manual curb mass, 46/54 weight distribution, 185/60HR14 tires, 10.8 US gallon tank, geometry, 7500 rpm redline and 1000 rpm idle. An archived factory bulletin supplies C52 five-speed and final/reverse reductions. UK power, mass and weight distribution are deliberately not substituted. UK steering and clearance are explicitly marked regional assumptions; handling, tire pressures and torque interpolation away from published anchors are estimates.

Native editor roundtrip, production launch, eight-angle render review and 390/1280 cockpit review are release requirements. No map/world/race edits.
