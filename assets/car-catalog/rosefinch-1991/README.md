# Rosefinch 1991

Original logo-free 2994 raspberry micro roadster and RHD cockpit, inspired by the 1991 Japanese Honda Beat E-PP1 E07A five-speed. Honda launch factbook supplies all ratios, power/torque, dimensions,760kg mass and different front/rear tires. Manufacturer states .440m unladen CG and43/57 distribution with one occupant: these conditions are documented rather than treated as identical measurements.

Metric PS and kgf-m converted to mechanical horsepower and lb-ft. Engine curve uses verified8100rpm power and7000rpm torque anchors; interpolation and all other unsourced dynamics are modeling estimates. Explicit155/65R13 front and165/60R14 rear tire configuration. Fixed rear camera, shared steering wheel, calibrated instrument area and existing surface-sensitive smoke/spray. No maps/worlds changed. Actual eight-angle plus390/1280 cockpit QA required before release.
