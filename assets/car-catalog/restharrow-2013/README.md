# Restharrow 2013

Original badge-free 2994 interpretation of the April 2013 French Dacia Duster Lauréate Plus dCi 110 4x4, K9K THP 898 1461 cc Euro 5 diesel, TL8 000 six-speed manual and left-hand drive. The original manufacturer brochure is retained. Its selected 4x4 column verifies 80 kW at 4000 rpm, 240 Nm at 1750 rpm, 1294 kg running order with 766/528 kg axle loads, 50 L fuel, 168 km/h and 12.5 seconds to 100 km/h. This excludes the 79 kW 4x2 column, UK-market variants, dCi 90, Prestige Look pack and later facelift. The brochure's rounded 110 ch is not treated as 110 mechanical bhp.

Dimensions, 210 mm clearance and black roof rails follow the brochure. The 1695 mm height includes roof rails; body-only height is 1625 mm. Standard 16-inch alloys are verified. The brochure's numerical tire field is blank, so 215/65R16 and its nominal radius remain explicit assumptions. The six published road speeds per 1000 rpm constrain the simulation ratios; the 4.857 final-drive split, individual reductions and reverse ratio are estimates, not claimed factory ratios. Fixed 50:50 AWD approximates the selectable coupling. Generic turbocharged combustion, sound, fuel and thermal behavior approximate the diesel without simulating its DPF or original control logic.

Original initial, spacing-pass and final body atlases, original dashboard and prompts are preserved. The two round instrument pods carry live speed/RPM, with gear and fuel, animated steering and wipers. The fitted gauges and shared replacement steering wheel are fictional 2994 presentation details. The four round dashboard vents and basic radio/climate console retain the selected pre-facelift character. OEM scans are references only; no manufacturer image pixels are used in gameplay.
