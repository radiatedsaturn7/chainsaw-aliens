# Lunaria 30 1991

Original badge-free 2994 mid-engine coupe based on the stock US 1991 Acura NSX 3.0 five-speed manual. The official [manufacturer press kit](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-4edb8848ab8a9f2f9a24ee004c34ca7a-1991-acura-nsx-introduction) supplies the manual engine, transmission, metric geometry, mass distribution and staggered tires. Automatic and later 3.2-liter specifications are excluded. The catalog's prior NSX mention was contextual research for Redbud, not an NSX entry; saved catalog aliases were checked before selection.

`research.json` separates factory anchors from gameplay estimates. Constant steering approximates the factory variable ratio; suspension, differential coefficients, engine interpolation and display performance are not calibrated factory behavior. Auto Assist operates the sourced five-speed manual and is not represented as the original four-speed automatic. The game does not claim faithful VTEC, traction-control or ABS behavior.

Original coral/charcoal body and blank LHD dashboard use native importer processing, measured shared wheels and live speed/RPM/gear/fuel instruments. Fixed rear camera and existing surface effects are preserved. Eight exterior views, moving desktop/handheld previews and readable cockpit composition are reviewed using the production renderer. These checks are not full campaign acceptance or acceleration calibration.

Local addition atop preserved Tansy f0673553; no existing vehicle, map or lighting edits. Original generation paths are retained in generation.json.
