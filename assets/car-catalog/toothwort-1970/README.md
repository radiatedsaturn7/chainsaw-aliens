# Toothwort 1970: oval wing-car Legend

Local-only original tribute to Richard Petty’s #43 Plymouth Superbird victory in the Carolina 500 at North Carolina Motor Speedway, Rockingham, on 8 March 1970. No equivalent local provenance or reserved Toothwort alias existed before import. This is a competition tribute, not a road 440 Superbird or a modern replica.

## Evidence and its limits
- [NASCAR’s complete Petty win record](https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2022/07/02/cataloging-all-200-of-richard-pettys-nascar-wins-one-by-one/), win102, explicitly identifies this event as Petty’s first #43 Superbird victory, over492laps. This is the authoritative event anchor.
- [NASCAR Hall of Fame](https://www.nascarhall.com/blog/pettys-prime-years) confirms the slope-nosed, tall-wing Superbird was used on faster tracks during1970. Not all18 Petty wins that season were Superbird wins.
- [Mecum’s primary restored Petty NASCAR-car record](https://www.mecum.com/lots/379965/1970-plymouth-superbird-richard-petty-nascar/) describes a426 race Hemi V8, four-speed manual, Nichels cage and steel racing wheels, with authentication attributed to Petty and team members. It is seller-provided restored-car evidence, not an original Rockingham build sheet. No claim is made that this documented surviving chassis was the specific Carolina500 winner. Conflicting rear-end descriptions and restored superspeedway speed claims are not treated as event facts.
