# Nightcoil GT 1995 research import

Original fictional AWD coupe. Reference: US 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse GSX five-speed manual, not the GS-T, automatic, later 17-inch GSX, or Fast & Furious hero car.

Manufacturer technical manual sections 0-18 and 2-5 support 1415 kg curb mass, dimensions and W5M33 gearing. Engine 210 hp at 6000 rpm, 214 lb-ft at 3000 rpm and 215/55R16 tires remain explicitly secondary-sourced. The import provenance lists sources, conflicts and every simulation-only assumption.

The effective final reduction is derived from primary and secondary tooth counts; the rear transfer ratio is not multiplied into all wheels. Body and dashboard are original artwork; steeringwheel is shared. Eight wheel-opening anchors are calibrated against source alpha. Tests and isolated build do not publish this car. Browser/rendered driving QA and primary corroboration of output/tires remain pending.
