# Mignonette 2011

Original badge-free 2994 interpretation of the standard UK/European 2011 Lexus LFA: naturally aspirated 4805 cc V10, rear-wheel drive and six-speed automated sequential gearbox. Right-hand-drive cockpit. Excludes the Nürburgring Package, racing cars, prototypes and later electric concept.

## Source boundaries

Preserved Lexus UK original technical release and Toyota global launch announcement establish 552 bhp at 8700 rpm, 480 Nm at 6800 rpm, 9000 rpm maximum, 48:52 weight distribution, dimensions and staggered 265/35 ZR20 front / 305/30 ZR20 rear tires. Toyota identifies the engine as 1LR-GUE; the conflicting UK table suffix is not adopted. Lexus production history confirms December 2010–December 2012 production and European delivery during 2011.

1480 kg is the manufacturer's **minimum kerb mass**; the source table spans 1480–1580 kg. The model uses that minimum, without claiming the exact weight of an optioned UK chassis or adding a driver. Toyota's separate 412 kW / 560 PS presentation is retained without mixing metric and mechanical horsepower.

Six forward reductions are sourced: 3.231, 2.188, 1.609, 1.233, 0.970, 0.795. The separate 1.259 counter reduction multiplies the 3.417 differential into the runtime's single effective final reduction, 4.302003. A first-gear road-speed test checks this against the maker's approximately 52 mph at 9000 rpm. Reverse is explicitly an unverified estimate.
