# Bugle 1966: Daytona endurance Legend

Local-only original tribute to Ken Miles / Lloyd Ruby’s 1966 Daytona 24 Hours winner, Ford GT40 Mk II P/1015 (#98), 6 February 1966. Not a road GT40, Mk I, replica or subsequent Le Mans build. No equivalent provenance or reserved Bugle alias existed at import.

## Evidence and limits
- [Shelby American Collection, P/1015](https://www.shelbyamericancollection.org/gt40s/1966-gt40-p1015): exact chassis, crew, result, 485-horsepower 427 V8 and Kar Kraft T44 four-speed; rear brake-cooling ducts. Its dry-sump engine installation and Le Mans rebuilding are described as later work, not Daytona specification.
- [Ford history](https://corporate.ford.com/corporate/corporate/articles/history/ford-gt40-origins-le-mans-committee-victory/): corroborates 427 Mk II, short nose, four-speed and Daytona victory.
- [The Henry Ford photograph](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/444381): contemporary #98 GT40 Mark II and Miles/Ruby win, dated February 5–6.

The 7.0 L displacement rounds 427 cubic inches. Mid-engine, rear-drive architecture and four manual gears distinguish this competition car. 1100 kg, 475 lb-ft, complete RPM/torque curve, 195 mph and 4.4-second 0–60 targets, geometry, aero, gearing, suspension, brakes and 35-gallon tank are simulation estimates, not measured Daytona setup. Museum 210 mph is itself estimated, not an event speed. Equal 245/60R15 tires are a native approximation, not exact historical stagger. Auto Assist is accessibility, not a factory automatic. No ABS/TC fitted by default.

## Original art and fit
Original image-generated aubergine/copper/cream eight-view shell with short nose, roof-integrated doors, side intakes, rear-deck ducts and round rear lamps. No historic stripes, sponsors, numbers, logos or third-party assets. Magnesium-style wheels and a low right-hand-drive cockpit with outer-sill lever use separate native art references. Source PNGs and prompts retained with SHA-256 provenance. Native opacity normalization applies only to the decoded wheel import copy. Individually measured wheel anchors cover original empty arches without altering physical tire radius. Live speed/RPM/gear/fuel fit the right-hand blank binnacle, with shared steering wheel below it. Digital telemetry, shared wipers and generic engine audio are game conventions.
