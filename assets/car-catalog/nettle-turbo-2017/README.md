# Nettle Turbo 2017

Original logo-free 2994 compact two-seat roadster with weathered wine-red exterior and individually calibrated LHD cockpit. Inspired by the US 2017 124 Spider Abarth six-speed manual, with sport-mode peak torque anchors. Genuine eight-angle PNGs, shared runtime tires and steering wheel, fixed-rear camera and surface smoke.

US OEM specs supply 1124 kg curb mass, 164 SAE hp at 5500 rpm, 184 lb-ft at 2500 rpm in Sport, 6250 rpm limiter, manual ratios and 3.454 final drive, dimensions, 45 L tank, 15.5 steering ratio and 205/45R17 tires. European 1060 kg empty mass, 170 metric hp and 6500 rpm are explicitly not substituted. The OEM turbo pressure line pairs gauge and absolute units; runtime boost uses 22 psi gauge converted to bar, not 2.49 bar gauge. Sport-mode controller switching and exact mechanical LSD control are approximations.

Handling assumptions and display performance targets are documented in research.json, not claimed as measured simulation or factory values. Release requires exact-head CI, native editor roundtrip, production launch and actual eight-angle plus 390/1280 cockpit review. No maps, worlds or races edited.
