# Sundew 660 1991

An original, logo-free 2994 salvage kei roadster inspired by the Japanese 1991 Cappuccino E-EA11R CAXF, not its later K6A or UK-export variants. The new teal body and right-hand-drive cockpit are fictional artwork. All eight body views have transparent wheel openings and reuse runtime tires; the cockpit reuses the existing steering wheel and individually placed instrument overlays.

Suzuki's official one-page specification supplies dimensions, 700 kg mass, 657 cc engine, 64 metric PS at 6,500 rpm, 8.7 kgf·m at 4,000 rpm, all five forward ratios, reverse, final drive, and 165/65R14 tires. Metric PS and kgf·m are converted rather than mislabeled as mechanical horsepower or newton-metres.

Remaining dynamics, redline/limiter, suspension, steering, drag, pressure, performance displays and collider details are explicitly modeling assumptions. No claim of measured acceleration or a functioning speed governor. Fixed rear camera and existing surface-responsive smoke/spray are retained.

Import through the native car editor serializer. No maps or worlds change. Exact-head runtime screenshots must pass review before release. Source facts are in research.json; field attribution and estimates are in import.json; original generation prompts are in prompts.json.
