# Angelica 1999

Original badge-free 2994 compact utility inspired by the standard 1999 US Isuzu VehiCROSS 3.5 V6, four-speed automatic and Torque-on-Demand AWD on 16-inch tires. No Ironman package, Japanese 3.2 engine or later 18-inch wheels. Current and staged source aliases and the old eleven-model crosswalk were checked; no overlap found.

The period April 1999 Car and Driver road test supplies the selected engine, dimension, tire and measured test-car weight anchors. The 1,778 kg mass rounds that test vehicle's 3,920 lb; it is not presented as a universal manufacturer curb mass. Public original brochure scans and the period interior photograph are preserved for visual reference. The brochure's tiny numerical table was not treated as legible verification. Exact primary-source ratios were not verified: individual/final/reverse gearing is explicitly estimated. TOD's variable torque distribution is approximated by a fixed rear-biased on-road setting; low range is not separately simulated. Other estimates are listed in import.json.

Original generated body/dashboard PNGs and prompts are retained. Eight individually fitted exterior directions use shared animated wheel layers. The sculpted dashboard follows the period radio-above-vents arrangement with fitted live speed, RPM, gear and fuel displays and the shared animated steering wheel. Simplified climate controls and salvage instruments are fictional 2994 replacements. A visual-review correction moved this car's parked wipers clear of its center stack; initial evidence remains labelled initial.

Keyboard and touch tests use production simulation and in-memory test routes. The real static picker smoke checks its initial visible art, navigates to Angelica on a later page, waits for its exterior art, then opens its selected cockpit and drives. No saved race, runtime or world file is changed.
