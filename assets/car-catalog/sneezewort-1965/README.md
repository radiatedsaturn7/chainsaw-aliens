# Sneezewort 1965

Playable fictional 2994 restoration of the 1965 US Chevrolet Corvair Monza 10537 two-door Sport Coupe with the standard 164 cu in engine, three-speed manual and 3.27 axle. This is the standard 95 gross-hp configuration; gameplay uses the separately published **78 net hp at 3600 rpm and 140 net lb-ft at 2400 rpm**. It is not the 110-hp option or turbocharged Corsa. Original GM documentation distinguishes gross and net; these period values are not labeled DIN/CEE or modern certified SAE net.

The original Chevrolet information kit supplies the 2530-lb ready-to-drive curb weight (935 front/1595 rear), standard ratios 3.22/1.84/1, reverse3.22, dimensions, steering ratio, tank and tire data. Page images and original PDF are retained under `references/`; the original sales brochure's Monza spread informs body and cockpit. Full source fields, access notes and modeling assumptions are in `import.json`. The 165/80R13 profile approximates the original 6.50x13 crossply; the loaded rolling radius comes from published862 rev/mile. Acceleration/top-speed targets, aerodynamics, curve interpolation, limits, suspension and collision geometry are estimates.

The original generated body atlas and its spacing revision are both preserved. All eight exterior views use the existing animated tire rig. The vehicle-specific left-hand dashboard has live speed, RPM, gear and fuel widgets and the shared animated steering wheel. The wheel was raised after the initial driving review; both initial and final evidence are retained. No manufacturer logo or reference-image pixels enter the game artwork.
