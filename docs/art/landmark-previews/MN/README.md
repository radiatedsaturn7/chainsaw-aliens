# Minneapolis landmark approaches (2994)

The untouched race keeps its existing bright world and futuristic skyline. The
native desktop (960×540) and handheld (390×844) playtest frames use a placeholder
car. The Institute of Art passes at 45 m, Walker Art Center at 85 m, and Basilica
of Saint Mary at 135 m. Guthrie Theater's native desktop view at 175 m is retained
as diagnostic evidence; the handheld bridge silhouette lacks separation from
the foreground and background in reviewed approaches.

The original road and scenery are unchanged. Approach distances refer to the
final collision-safe projection, not the source GPS mileage.
