# Chicago, IL — Landmark Run

Reviewed full native desktop and handheld at 30, 45, 65, 105, 175, 265 and 325 m. Navy Pier passes at 175 m on both: the damaged Ferris wheel, spokes and support legs read unmistakably against the authored sky. Wrigley tower crown is obscured by the handheld pause control at its stronger approaches and its bridge becomes too small farther back. Art Institute and Union Station facades are unreadable. Original lighting, HUD, and route unchanged.
