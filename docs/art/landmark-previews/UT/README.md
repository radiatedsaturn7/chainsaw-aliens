# Salt Lake City, UT — Landmark Run

Reviewed full native desktop and handheld at 45, 65, 105, 175, 265 and 325 m. Temple Square passes at desktop 65 m and handheld 45 m: multiple distinctive spires are plainly separated from the sky and HUD. Capitol dome is often obscured by the handheld pause control and its facade is dark; the depot and City and County Building lack readable architectural detail. Those three remain pending.
