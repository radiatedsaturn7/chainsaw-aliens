# Charleston, WV — Landmark Run

Reviewed full native desktop and handheld at 45, 65, 105, 175, 265 and 325 m. Clay Center passes at 105 m on both for distinct rounded roof shells and the exposed lattice. West Virginia State Capitol passes at 65 m on both for the lantern dome, broad wings and open ruined wall sections visible against the original night sky. Terrain masks most of the Haddad amphitheater, and Capitol Market loses its lower facade in shadow; those two remain pending.
