# New York landmark approaches (2994)

These are playtest frames from the native renderer at the route’s original morning
lighting. The test fixture loads the exact saved race document and uses a
placeholder car; desktop is 960×540 and handheld is 390×844.

- **Flatiron Building:** 45 m before projected placement, clear in both views.
  The source side could not produce a safe approach; the opposite side is a
  visual approximation. The original reference remains in the ledger.
- **New York Public Library:** 115 m in both views. The intact/broken lions
  and columned arches read most clearly on desktop; the handheld façade is
  smaller but unoccluded. Retains the exact 70-character saved art reference.
- **Rockefeller Center:** 195 m desktop / 175 m handheld. The tower crown and
  grounded base remain inside frame and clear of the handheld overlay.
- **Grand Central Terminal:** clear clock, entrance arches and pediment at
  75 m in both modes. The sharp bend creates a narrower visibility window.

All four collision volumes were checked against the original road and
continuation segments; no roads, lighting, or existing props were edited.
